Deep inside, something is chasing me — but it isn't clear in my mind what it is. I feel it, but I'm unable to articulate it. Maybe I'm chasing a question that isn't yet defined, or maybe I simply don't have enough knowledge or wisdom today to understand it.

This isn't spiritual or religious — it's something triggered by my own experiences and my interactions with the world around me. Maybe, with growing age, my thoughts are trying to make sense of things around me and rationalize why certain things happen only the way they do.

I know the question is still vague, the thoughts are vague, and this conversation is still vague. I'm trying to clear that ambiguity and articulate it for my own understanding.

The question that comes up is: what experiences or incidents are triggering these thoughts? I think that's a good starting point — at least it gives me a direction to infer my own thinking.

Though I don't yet have a list of those situations, I will start writing a series of blogs on the incidents or experiences as I encounter or recollect them. In each one, I'll try to explain the situation, the outcome, the decisions or factors that led to that outcome, and the triggers that grabbed my attention to evaluate it. Here is the framework I will follow:

Situation → Outcome → Influencers (rational, cultural, emotional…) → Internal Trigger Factors

I hope this will, over time, help clear my inner thoughts by revealing common patterns across the blogs. Thanks for sticking with me this far — I hope you'll join me on this journey of self-discovery. See you soon in my next blog.
